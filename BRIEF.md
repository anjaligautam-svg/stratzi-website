# Stratzi — Project Brief

> Single source of truth for what Stratzi is and what this website needs to communicate.

## Company

- **Name**: Stratzi (stratzi.ai)
- **Positioning**: The **AI-native Business Operating System** for SaaS-enabled MSMEs. NOT an AI chatbot. NOT a workflow tool. NOT a one-time automation. A full AI operations layer that sits on top of existing SaaS.
- **Audience**: MSMEs in India, primarily BFSI / Professional Services / Logistics. Buyers are skeptical ops/founder-CEOs who want compounding intelligence, not another tool.
- **Voice / tone**: Editorial, confident, plain-spoken. Avoid SaaS-template language ("Unlock exponential growth", "AI-powered solutions"). First-person or second-person is OK.
- **Primary CTA**: Book a discovery call (30 min)
- **Secondary CTA**: See it in action / view a case study

## The Three Pillars (core positioning — every section must ladder back to these)

1. **Company Brain** — institutional memory captured, structured, and queryable. Grows smarter as the business operates.
2. **Agentic Employees** — custom AI agents that own entire workflows (not single tasks). Navigate portals, communicate with clients, hand off only on real decisions.
3. **Learning Loops** — every agent action sharpens the brain. Month 12 is measurably more capable than month 1.
